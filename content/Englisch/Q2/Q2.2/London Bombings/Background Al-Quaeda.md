Al-Qaeda is a global terrorist organization with a Salafist jihadist ideology that aims to establish a pan-Islamic caliphate and expel Western influence from Muslim countries. Founded in the late 1980s by Osama bin Laden and other militants, al-Qaeda is known for carrying out high-profile attacks, including the 9/11 attacks in the United States. The group has faced significant military and intelligence pressure from the international community, leading to a decline in its influence in recent years. However, al-Qaeda's affiliated groups continue to pose a threat in various regions, and the organization remains a symbol of violent extremism and terrorism.
