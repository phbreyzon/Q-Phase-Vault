### Opinion and reaction

In _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ [Tariq Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_Ali "Tariq Ali") and [Robert Fisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Fisk "Robert Fisk") in [The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent"), echoing earlier comments by [George Galloway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Galloway "George Galloway") in parliament, claimed that the [Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War "Iraq War") was the cause of the attacks, though both also ran opinion pages and letters responding that such claims were too oversimplified, and the general mood of the press was that Britain was always a terrorist target and at most the war made Britain a more prominent target.

The American [Fox News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_News "Fox News") network was criticised for its response to the news. [Brian Kilmeade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Kilmeade "Brian Kilmeade") claimed that the attacks worked to the advantage of conservatives, putting terrorism back on the political agenda at a time when the [G8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G8 "G8") were discussing [climate change and poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_and_poverty "Climate change and poverty"). Washington managing editor, [Brit Hume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Hume "Brit Hume"), claimed his first response to the news was that it was "time to buy", referring to the [stock market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market "Stock market"), and host [John Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gibson_(political_commentator) "John Gibson (political commentator)") quipped that if the [International Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee "International Olympic Committee") "picked France instead of London to hold the Olympics, it would have been the one time we could look forward to where we didn't worry about terrorism. [Terrorists would] blow up Paris, and who cares?
