-  He expresses his concerns regarding teaching school students about the british Empire, since they invaded so many countries, which is not something to be proud of.
- l.47 ...in many countries the memories are somewhat bitter, and revenge is exacted to this day
- l. 55 but not all memories are bad. Some colonized believe the britons brought education and development to their countries. They refer to the empire as a tough-but-fair former school teacher.

### Stichpunkte 
- British wealth and power was build on top of slavery 
- Half of the 60 banking firms listed in 1835 have been noted in the slave compensation records.
- Many British multinationals, such as Elementis, the chemicals and personal care business, have roots in 19th century merchant firms operating in the empire
- The power structures of the British Empire, explain some of our modern inequalities
- It is important to remember that even at the time, the way that colossal wealth was reaped from the empire was a source of shame. british families that became rich from the colonising of India and slave plantations often concealed the fact
