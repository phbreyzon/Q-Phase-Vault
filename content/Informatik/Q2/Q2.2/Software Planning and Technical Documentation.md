
## Software Planning 

In here you ask yourself the question: *What should the produkt look like?*

Hereby you need to distinguish between two things: 

- **Functional requirements**
- **Non-functional requirements**

### UX Documentation 

One of the measures you might take in order to improve the user experience, is to know with whom are you dealing with as a customer in the first place. 

Once you found out who you target person is, you might take a look into userscenarios.
